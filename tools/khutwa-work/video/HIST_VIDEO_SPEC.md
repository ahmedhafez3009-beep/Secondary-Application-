# «خُطوة» History explainer video — script spec (pilot)

Lesson: Baccalaureate grade 2 History, Unit 1, Lesson 1 «بناء الدولة المصرية واستمرارها عبر التاريخ».
Source: ONLY the clean OCR of the ministry book, /home/claude/khutwa/ocr/b2hi1.txt ("=== pN" page markers). Find the lesson's pages (and the unit opener) yourself; cite page numbers.
Narrator: the app owner, Egyptian, records in his own voice. Audience: 16–17-year-old Baccalaureate students.

## Format (Akhdar single-sheet whiteboard)
- One whiteboard sheet for the whole video; 5–7 slides ("boards"); each board stays on the sheet; camera zooms out after each board to show all previous ones, then zooms in on the next.
- A pinned panel «ملخص الشريحة» with 2–4 short bullets per board; each bullet appears on the word that carries it.
- Bottom subtitle bar: Egyptian colloquial + English.
- Target length 7:00–8:30 → about 620–760 spoken Arabic words total (≈0.65 s/word incl. pauses). Ahmed asked to widen the video: nothing a student must memorize or may be examined on may be left out.

## Structure
1. Hook (≤15 s): a surprising question or contrast from the lesson itself (no greeting first).
2. Questions map: «هات ورقة وقلم واكتب ورايا…» 3–4 questions the video answers (they become the boards).
3. Body: one board per question. Everyday comparison first when it helps (home, school, a family business), then the book's content, then the term + «يعني بالبلدي».
4. A re-hook every 45–60 s: question to the viewer, a «وقّف الفيديو وفكّر» 3-second pause, or a surprising fact from the book.
5. Exam corner: cover the book's own exam-style prompts for this lesson (علل / ماذا لو / قارن / فسّر) — at least 2, answered from the book.
6. Summary: the questions again with one-line answers (last board = summary while camera shows the whole sheet).
7. Close: «حل كويز الدرس في خُطوة» + one comment question.

## Content rules
- Every date, name, number, place and claim must be stated in the book text; put the page number next to it in the script's notes column. If the book doesn't state it, leave it out.
- Follow the book's framing on political/national topics; no personal opinions; neutral wording.
- Cover ALL terms the lesson defines (no cap), each defined in the book's words; keep the book's definitions word for word when you define a term (quote ≤ 15 words).
- Spoken sentences ≤ 18 words, Cairene colloquial (تانية، كده، بس، يعني، إزاي، ليه), not MSA; terms stay in فصحى as in the book. Numbers written as spoken.
- No real photos or drawings of real historical people's faces from the book; boards use original drawings: maps of Egypt and the Nile, timeline arrows, simple icons (crown, pyramid, scroll, shield), drawn generic figures.
