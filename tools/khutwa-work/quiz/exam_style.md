# Egyptian Baccalaureate (البكالوريا المصرية) – Grade 2 exam style: research notes

Compiled 2026-11-16 from web research. Labels used throughout:
- **[OFFICIAL]** = moe.gov.eg document or page read directly.
- **[PRESS-official]** = press report that attributes the item to the Ministry, its National Center for Examinations, or a ministry source.
- **[PRESS]** = press or education site with no clear ministry attribution.
- **[NOT FOUND]** = searched for and not located.

Access note: moe.gov.eg spec pages list subjects but return no document links to the fetcher. `ellibrary.moe.gov.eg`
(the official Baccalaureate curriculum library) was not reachable from this session. Some official PDFs may therefore
exist but could not be checked.

## 0. Global rules for Baccalaureate grade 2 (2026/2027), applying to every subject

- **Final exam format: 50% MCQ / 50% essay (مقالي).** [PRESS-official] The source is a ministerial decree reported after the
  Supreme Council for Pre-University Education meeting. It applies to Baccalaureate grades 2 and 3 and to Thanaweya Amma from
  2026/2027. https://www.masrawy.com/news/education-schooleducation/details/2026/8/17/3034277 ; https://vetogate.com/5762421
- **Marks:** each subject is out of 100. The total is 600 (Arabic, first foreign language, history, the track elective,
  plus the grade-3 subjects). The best attempt per subject counts. [PRESS-official, same sources]
- **Sittings for grade 2:** the May sitting is compulsory and free. An optional July sitting costs 200 EGP per subject. [PRESS-official]
- **Grade-2 subjects** (Ministerial Decision 195, 11 Aug 2026), 6 periods/week each: Arabic, first foreign language, and
  Egyptian history are common to all tracks. One elective per track: Medicine track takes Physics or Math. Engineering takes
  Chemistry or Programming & AI. Business takes Accounting or Business Administration. Arts takes Psychology or a second
  foreign language (e.g. French or German). Financial literacy and PE are not added to the total. [PRESS-official]
  https://www.masrawy.com/news/education-schooleducation/details/2026/8/20/3035635
- **Coursework gate:** a student needs 60% of the year-work marks to sit the exam. Year work is scored out of 100: weekly
  assessment 20, monthly test 1 20, monthly test 2 20, project 15, homework 15, behaviour 10. [PRESS-official]
- **Monthly tests (Nov 2026) for grade-2 Baccalaureate:** 50% MCQ / 50% essay. Each school must set three different
  versions per subject. Content is limited to what was taught that month. The press says the essays measure "الصياغة
  والتعبير" (wording and expression) and "الفهم والتحليل" (understanding and analysis). [PRESS-official, Ministry
  general-education sector circular] https://shbabbek.com/show/287303
- **Cognitive-level split for the Baccalaureate:** [NOT FOUND]. No official percentages for the Baccalaureate have been
  published as far as I could find.
- **Closest official benchmark (Thanaweya Amma 2026, grade 3):** 70% simple+medium / 30% higher levels. Format was
  85% MCQ / 15% essay. [PRESS-official, ministry source, Apr 2026]
  https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/236855/
  An older ministry statement (2024) gave a difficulty mix of 30% below-average / 40% average / 30% distinguished.
  https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/36921/
- **Grade-2 Baccalaureate subject specs (مواصفات), نماذج استرشادية, and an official بنك أسئلة:** [NOT FOUND] as of
  Nov 2026. Question "banks" circulating for grade-2 Baccalaureate (e.g. history) are teacher-made, not ministry ones. [PRESS]

**Practical implication:** each paper should be about half MCQ (4 options, one correct) and half written. The written half
has short answers, explanations, and calculations with steps shown.

## 1. Arabic (اللغة العربية)
- Baccalaureate grade 2: 50/50 global rule only; per-branch spec [NOT FOUND].
- Closest guidance, Thanaweya Amma 2026: 55 questions, 80 marks. [PRESS-official] Sources disagree on the split:
  - Youm7 / guide model: 54 MCQ + 1 essay.
  - Ministry source to Telegraph: 51 MCQ + 4 essay.
- The ministry says the questions target comprehension and analysis, not rote memorization. [PRESS-official]
- Branch breakdown (نحو/بلاغة/أدب/قصة/تعبير), marks per branch, and duration: [NOT FOUND] in reachable sources.
- Typical stems: [NOT FOUND officially]. Common Egyptian style (unverified, general practice): a passage followed by MCQ on
  meaning, i'rab, and rhetorical device, plus a short written answer.
- Sources: https://www.youm7.com/7325963 ; https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/236855/

## 2. English (first foreign language)

## 3. History (التاريخ – Egyptian/national history in the Baccalaureate)
- Baccalaureate grade 2: 50/50 rule only; spec [NOT FOUND].
- Closest, Thanaweya Amma 2026: 46 questions = 44 MCQ + 2 essay, 60 marks. [PRESS-official]
- Essay types in a teacher-made grade-2 Baccalaureate history bank [PRESS, not ministry]:
  - understanding/analysis/interpretation
  - comparison (قارن)
  - opinion (رأيك)
  - inference from a historical text
  - application
- Paraphrased examples from that bank:
  - "Define the modern state and state its four elements."
  - "Explain what a state's geographic space means and name its parts."
- Sources: https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/236855/ ; https://shbabbek.com/show/284445

## 4. Physics (فيزياء)
- Baccalaureate grade 2: 50/50 rule only; spec [NOT FOUND].
- Closest, Thanaweya Amma 2026: 46 questions, 60 marks. [PRESS-official]
  - 32 MCQ × 1 mark
  - 12 MCQ × 2 marks
  - 2 essays × 2 marks
  - All MCQ have 4 options. A concept/formula booklet (كتيب المفاهيم) is given in the exam.
  - Levels: 70% simple/medium, 30% higher.
- Official grade-11 model (moe.gov.eg, 2020/21 monthly test) [OFFICIAL]: 4-option MCQ, mostly calculation and
  graph/diagram based (manometer, pressure–depth graph, density–mass graph), with a formulas page attached.
  - Example (paraphrased): "A tangential force of 200 N acts on the face of a 10 cm cube; find the pressure."
    "zero" is one of the options. My reading, not from the source: this is a conceptual trap, since a tangential
    force produces no pressure.
- Sources: https://filgamaa.com/%d9%85%d9%88%d8%a7%d8%b5%d9%81%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%aa%d8%ad%d8%a7%d9%86%d8%a7%d8%aa-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%ab%d8%a7%d9%86%d9%88%d9%8a%d8%a9-%d8%a7%d9%84%d8%b9%d8%a7%d9%85%d8%a9-2026-%d8%b9%d8%af/ ;
  https://moe.gov.eg/media/uohpdbqx/06-g-11.pdf ; https://www.youm7.com/7449073

## 6. Mathematics (رياضيات)
- Baccalaureate grade 2: 50/50 rule only; spec [NOT FOUND].
- Closest, Thanaweya Amma 2026, pure math: 20 questions, 30 marks. [PRESS-official]
  - 10 × 1 mark
  - 8 × 2 marks
  - 2 essays × 2 marks, graded per correct step
  - Must include basic, medium, and advanced items so that it "separates" ability levels.
  - Applied math: same count, 30 marks.
- Essays require shown working (step marks).
- Source: https://www.youm7.com/7325188

## 7. Programming & AI (البرمجة والذكاء الاصطناعي)

  - Mix of "produce an answer" and "choose an answer" items, with images.

## 10. Psychology (علم النفس)
- Baccalaureate grade 2: 50/50 rule only; spec [NOT FOUND].
- Closest, old-system grade-2 Psychology & Sociology (National Center for Examinations, 2024) [PRESS-official]:
  - 21 MCQ + 2 short essays "صياغات متنوعة" (varied formulations).
  - About 87.5% objective / 12.5% essay, by marks.
  - Exam out of 24, plus 6 year-work marks, 30 total.
  - 2 hours, including 10 minutes reading.
  - **Levels: understanding 41.5% / application 41.5% / higher 17%.** This is the only explicit level split found.
- Situation/case stems (مواقف): common in this subject but [NOT FOUND] in an official spec text.
- Source: https://www.youm7.com/6533608

## Summary for quiz design (inference, flagged)
- Aim for about 50% 4-option MCQ (one correct answer, close distractors) and 50% written items. Written items cover:
  - short explanation (علل/فسر)
  - comparison (قارن)
  - consequence (ماذا يترتب/ماذا يحدث)
  - text or situation inference
  - multi-step calculation with step marks
- For the level mix, a defensible default from official precedents is about 70% basic/medium and 30% higher-order.
  Psychology precedent: 41.5% understanding / 41.5% application / 17% higher.
- Re-check `ellibrary.moe.gov.eg/EgyptianBaccalaureate` and moe.gov.eg for grade-2 Baccalaureate مواصفات and
  نماذج before the May 2027 exam. These had not been published or could not be reached at the time of writing.
